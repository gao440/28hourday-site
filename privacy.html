Privacy Policy

Effective July 15, 2026 · Applies to the 28 Hour Day iOS app and this website.

28 Hour Day is built to run on your phone, not on our servers. This policy explains the small amount of data we do handle, why, and what never leaves your device.

What stays on your device

  • Your blocked-app choices. App blocking uses Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) technology. Apple designs it so that which apps you select is represented by opaque tokens that are unreadable outside your device — we cannot see which apps you block, and this information is never transmitted anywhere.
  • Your schedule, goals, and personal statements. Your bedtime and wake time, your goals, and the personal reasons you write during setup are stored on your device.
  • Usage of the app itself. Night outcomes, unlock events, and check-in ratings are computed and stored locally.

What we collect, and why

  • Email address — collected when you create an account, used solely to sign you in and identify your backup. Authentication is provided by Supabase, our infrastructure provider. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the email address (or Apple's private relay address) those services share.
  • Streak and progress backup — your night history, streaks, morning check-ins, and (if provided) your first name are backed up to your account so they survive a reinstall. This backup is stored with Supabase, protected by row-level security so only your account can access it. Your blocked-app choices are not part of this backup — they cannot leave the device.
  • Purchase records — subscriptions are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to validate purchases; RevenueCat receives purchase receipt information and an anonymous app-user identifier. We never see your payment details.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no advertising SDKs.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites, and no sale of data.
  • No reading of your Screen Time activity — the app schedules blocking; it does not receive your browsing or app-usage history.

Subscriptions

28 Hour Day Pro is offered as an auto-renewing subscription ($29.99/year with a 7-day free trial, or $5.99/month; prices may vary by region). Payment is charged to your Apple ID. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and can be managed or cancelled in your device's Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Terms of use: Apple's standard EULA.

Deleting your data

  • Delete your account from inside the app (Settings → Account → Delete Account). This permanently deletes your sign-in and your cloud backup.
  • Delete the app to remove all on-device data.

Children

28 Hour Day is designed for adults managing their own sleep and is not directed at children. It is not a parental-control product and provides no monitoring of anyone else's device.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we'll update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
