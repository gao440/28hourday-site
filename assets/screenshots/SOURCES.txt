28 Hour Day website screenshots
Captured October 9, 2026 from the current SwiftUI app, version 1.0 (7).

All three originals are 1320 x 2868 PNGs from an iPhone 17 Pro Max simulator
running iOS 26.5. WebP copies are resized and compressed for page loading.
No UI was generated, reconstructed, or painted over.

schedule.png: Settings > Sleep Schedule. The homepage uses a CSS crop of
the real bedtime, wake-time, and active-night controls.
check-in.png: The normal Morning Check-in flow, opened through the app's
debug testing menu. No answer was entered.
nights.png: Nights with example history from the app's built-in Seed Demo
Stats action. The page labels this example history and shows a CSS crop
of the summary. These numbers are not user outcomes or measured sleep.

The full originals remain available through the image links. They preserve
simulator setup warnings. These captures show the app interface; simulator
captures do not establish that Screen Time blocking works on a real iPhone.

The capture build included the existing October 9 working-tree changes.
