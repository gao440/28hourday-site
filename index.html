How it works

Three steps, then it runs every night.

1

Pick your apps and your bedtime

Choose the apps that keep you up and the window you want protected. iOS Screen Time does the enforcing — this isn't an honor system.

2

The shield answers 2 AM for you

Reach for a blocked app at night and you meet a block screen carrying your own words — the reason you wrote for protecting your sleep. Need an exception? A mindful 30-minute unlock re-locks itself.

3

Mornings build the streak

A ten-second morning check-in shows what last night gave you: hours protected, streak growing, one honest insight. That's the loop that makes it stick.

Questions

Fair questions, straight answers.

When can I get it?

28 Hour Day is in the final stretch before App Store release. The download badge above will go live the day it ships.

Can't I just open the app anyway?

Blocking is enforced by Apple's Screen Time system, not by a reminder you can swipe away. There is a deliberate escape hatch — a 30-minute unlock that re-locks itself — because real life has exceptions. The difference is you'll make that choice looking at your own words, not on autopilot.

What does it cost?

28 Hour Day Pro is $29.99/year with a 7-day free trial, or $5.99/month. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled in your device Settings, and you can cancel anytime during the trial without being charged.

What happens to my data?

Your nights live on your phone. Which apps you block never leaves your device — Apple's Screen Time design makes that data invisible even to us. An account (email only) backs up your streak and stats so they survive a reinstall, and you can delete the account and its backup from inside the app at any time. No ads, no tracking. Details in the privacy policy.

Does it work with my sleep schedule?

You set your own bedtime and wake time, including schedules that cross midnight and different days of the week. The morning check-in follows your morning, whenever that is.
