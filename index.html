Questions

Fair questions, straight answers.

When can I get it?

Soon — it's in the final stretch before App Store release. The waitlist is the fastest way in: you'll get one email the day it ships, and founding members on the list lock in $19.99/year for life. No spam, and you can unsubscribe with one tap.

Can't I just open the app anyway?

Blocking is enforced by Apple's Screen Time system, not by a reminder you can swipe away. There is a deliberate escape hatch — a 30-minute unlock that re-locks itself — because real life has exceptions. The difference is you'll make that choice looking at your own words, not on autopilot.

What does it cost?

28 Hour Day Pro is $29.99/year — about 8¢ a night — with a 7-day free trial, or $5.99/month. Waitlist founding members lock in $19.99/year instead. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled in your device Settings, and you can cancel anytime during the trial without being charged.

What happens to my data?

Your nights live on your phone. Which apps you block never leaves your device — Apple's Screen Time design makes that data invisible even to us. An account (email only) backs up your streak and stats so they survive a reinstall, and you can delete the account and its backup from inside the app at any time. No ads, no tracking. Details in the privacy policy.

Does it work with my sleep schedule?

You set your own bedtime and wake time, including schedules that cross midnight and different days of the week. The morning check-in follows your morning, whenever that is.
